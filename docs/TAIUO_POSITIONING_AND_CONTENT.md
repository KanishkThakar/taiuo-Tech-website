# Taiuo: beauty technology, clearly explained

Owner brief adopted 10 September 2026. This is the shared copy and production plan for the website, social profiles, presentations, outreach and creator briefs. Social posts and profile changes have not been published by this document.

## The description everyone uses

**Taiuo is an AI-powered beauty tech platform that helps you explore your skin, build a more informed care routine, and track visible changes over time.**

Short version: **Beauty technology for skin insights, informed routines and progress over time.**

Core message: **Taiuo helps you understand your skin—not just choose your next product.**

Taiuo builds the platform. Recommendations support the experience; a skincare or fragrance catalog is not the company identity. Do not introduce Taiuo as a skincare brand, beauty store, product collection or fragrance house.

## Website experience

1. Hero: “Understand your skin. Make smarter beauty decisions.” Use the shared description, primary **Start your skin analysis**, secondary **See how Taiuo works**. The primary action opens the existing scan flow; the secondary opens the on-page interface preview.
2. Four steps: **Scan → Understand → Make routine choices → Track changes.** Show the user's outcome at each step.
3. Interactive preview: skin insights, AM/PM routine and saved check-ins. Label sample data and illustrative interfaces beside the visual. Never make a campaign portrait appear to be an analysed user.
4. Why return: the continuity between check-ins and routine decisions. Start with products already owned; introduce Check your shelf as a contextual review, not a product safety guarantee.
5. Evidence and limitations: explain capture conditions and image-based estimates. No overall accuracy percentage, clinical validation, dermatologist approval or guaranteed result without suitable evidence.
6. Supporting recommendations: skincare and fragrance appear after the platform journey and evidence. No shopping CTA in the hero. Fragrance preferences come from the user, not a facial diagnosis.
7. FAQ and final scan invitation. No forced purchase, invented testimonials or artificial before/after results.

The premium cream, serif wordmark and restrained typography remain. Interface examples replace the full-width campaign photo and the early bottle/category grid. The two repositories use the same narrative; integrated app links stay on their origin, standalone website links open taiuo.com.

## Three pinned posts: ready-to-design scripts

### 1. What Taiuo is — 5-slide carousel

1. **Your skin. Better understood.** AI-powered beauty technology.
2. **Start with a guided selfie.** Explore what your photo shows.
3. **Turn insights into context.** Make more informed everyday routine choices.
4. **Build a record over time.** Compare saved check-ins under similar conditions.
5. **Understanding comes first.** Recommendations are optional. Start your skin analysis at taiuo.com.

Caption: “Meet Taiuo: an AI-powered beauty tech platform for skin insights, informed routines and progress over time. Start with a guided scan, explore your insights, then return with a clearer record of your skin. Image-based lifestyle insights, not a medical diagnosis. Start at taiuo.com.”

Visuals: wordmark, capture UI, one report panel and scan history. No product grid or invented customer quote.

### 2. How to use Taiuo — 25–35 second screen recording

Hook: **Your first Taiuo scan, step by step.**

Show: open taiuo.com → Start your skin analysis → actual consent and capture guidance → successful scan → one insight → routine → how to return to saved scans. Include real account requirements when the app asks for them; do not edit a login requirement out of the walkthrough. Cut waiting time only with “Processing time shortened.”

Caption: “A guided selfie. A clearer look at your insights. A routine to consider. Here’s how the Taiuo experience works—from your first scan to your next check-in. Use clear, even light and follow the capture guidance. Save your scans to compare them over time.”

### 3. Why tracking matters — 5-slide carousel

1. **One selfie is a moment. A record adds context.**
2. **Keep the conditions similar.** Lighting and camera differences can change what the image shows.
3. **Look beyond one number.** Read the details alongside your routine and how your skin feels.
4. **Change is not always improvement.** Scores can fluctuate; a comparison does not prove a product caused a change.
5. **Return to understand, not to chase a perfect score.** Your skin is not a beauty rating.

Caption: “Your skin journal is a reference, not a verdict. Taiuo keeps your check-ins together so you can look back with context. Similar capture conditions make comparisons more useful. Improvement is not guaranteed, and the app does not establish what caused a change.”

## Demonstration and creator acceptance

- Record on the release that viewers can access. Confirm the tested URL and date. Current code is not proof that the live server has deployed it.
- Show a complete successful session before publishing a “how it works” claim. Capture failures honestly; no fixture masquerading as live output.
- Obtain permission covering the person's image, skin report, channels and duration. Hide email, account ID, callback tokens, signed asset URLs and unrelated personal details. Keep a consent record and removal contact.
- Let users describe their experience in their own words. No scripted testimonials attributed to a real person, or numerical claims from a single example.
- Disclose paid or gifted creator partnerships visibly. The creator must demonstrate Taiuo, not imply medical expertise or endorse an invented Taiuo product.
- Report model component benchmarks only with test scope and limitations. No “94.8% accurate skin analysis”; that documented figure concerns a synthetic capture-quality test, not clinical accuracy.

## How to judge the positioning

Run a five-second homepage comprehension check with people unfamiliar with Taiuo. Ask “What is Taiuo?”, “What would you do first?” and “Why would you return?” Capture answers before explaining the product. Success means users describe skin understanding through technology, identify the scan action and understand saved check-ins. If they say “skincare store”, revise the hierarchy again.

Track landing-to-scan starts, completed scans, report views, saved check-ins and returning users where consented analytics exist. Keep denominators and observation windows explicit. These are measurement requirements, not claims that new analytics instrumentation has been implemented here. Likes and product clicks alone do not establish that the positioning works.

## Release status boundaries

Those require account access, actual captures and permissioned participants.
